About Arco Group

Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Las Vegas' Trusted Contractor

Founded in 2009 by master craftsmen with deep roots in the Las Vegas community, Arco Group began with a simple mission: deliver premium construction services with honesty, transparency, and uncompromising quality.

Over 15 years, we've grown from a small remodeling crew to a full-service general contracting firm handling everything from luxury home builds to emergency water damage restoration.

Our deep understanding of desert climate construction, local building codes, and Las Vegas permitting processes gives us an unmatched advantage.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our services.

Are you licensed and insured in Nevada?

Yes. Arco Group holds Nevada State Contractor License #0087654, is fully bonded, and carries $2M in liability insurance.

How long does a typical home remodel take?

A kitchen remodel typically takes 4-8 weeks, a bathroom 3-6 weeks, and whole-home renovations 3-6 months.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely. We provide free, no-obligation estimates with detailed line-item pricing.

What warranty do you offer?

We offer a lifetime workmanship warranty on all our projects, plus manufacturer warranties on all materials.
